# Global E-Commerce Revenue & Customer Performance Analysis
**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Power Query, Pivot Tables, Trend Forecasting, Customer Feedback Metrics)

## 💡 1. The Business Problem & Objectives
*   **Context:** This project analyzes global product sales data and customer feedback logs covering a transactional window from late 2023 to 2025. 
*   **Problem Statement:** While the product maintains steady global popularity, there are distinct regional fluctuations in purchase volume, localized drops in customer satisfaction, and predictable post-peak sales declines.
*   **Objectives:** 
    1. Evaluate regional sales density to optimize inventory and marketing allocation.
    2. Map out monthly purchase trends to understand and mitigate post-peak sales drops.
    3. Analyze customer feedback benchmarks across all regions to identify operational gaps.

## 🔍 2. Data Engineering & Cleaning
*   **Aggregation:** Summarized global purchase logs to track a total revenue of ₱4.9 million from 24,482 total products sold during the two-year period.
*   **Time-Series Segmentation:** Structured sales records by Year and Month (2024 vs. 2025) to isolate volume fluctuations and identify seasonal buying behavior.
*   **Sentiment Alignment:** Categorized and averaged customer feedback scores on a 1-to-5 scale to establish a clear quality-of-service baseline for each market.

*   **📉 Regional Valleys & Feedback Extremes:** Antarctica achieved the highest customer feedback score despite lower overall sales volume, indicating exceptional product satisfaction in a smaller niche market. Conversely, South America recorded the lowest feedback score, signaling critical issues with product quality or customer experience. Overall, Oceania and South America remain the lowest-performing markets.
*   **📅 Monthly Volume & Sales Drop Trends:** Product purchases demonstrate a consistent cyclical pattern where sales volume sharply declines immediately following a peak month. In 2025, the absolute best-selling month was May, reaching a peak of 1,352 products sold across all regions. However, the subsequent historical lows occurred in February (853 products), April (784 products), and June (696 products).

## 🚀 4. Strategic Recommendations
1. **Analyze Sales Drop Patterns:** Investigate the operational factors causing volume to tank immediately after peak months. Focus on identifying whether this pattern is driven by limited marketing follow-through, post-campaign stockouts, or natural seasonal buying behavior.
2. **Enhance Product Quality and Support:** Take immediate steps to improve product quality and customer service frameworks. Prioritize these updates heavily in South America to boost their low feedback scores and salvage the customer experience.
3. **Expand Underperforming Markets:** Strengthen marketing, local promotions, and brand awareness campaigns in South America and Oceania to convert them into high-growth channels.
4. **Leverage High-Performing Regions:** Continue reinforcing and expanding the successful logistics and sales strategies utilized in Africa and Asia. Use these high-performing regions as structural models for underperforming markets.
